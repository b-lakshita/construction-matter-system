Construction Matter System

A matter, limitation and compliance record for commercial construction suits before the Commercial Courts. The system prepares the record and the drafts; every operative step awaits counsel's confirmation and is entered in the audit record.

Scope of Automation

The system acts of its own motion in one instance only: the file locks against a written statement arriving after day 120, where the statute admits no discretion. Every other step awaits counsel.

Rules of Practice

The rules applied at intake — interrogatories by leave, originals for impounding, notice before application — are the practice's own, recorded and versioned. The intelligence applying them is rented and replaceable.

The Audit Record

Every position traces to a rule, a date, a document and a signature. Each entry names its actor — engine or counsel — and the record is append-only.

Section 12A — Pre-Institution Mediation

Facts capable of supporting urgency, as pleaded:
  • The plaint recites "grave and irreparable urgency" at ¶31 without particulars.
  • No interim relief is sought that could not await mediation.
  • The limitation period has more than eleven months left to run.
URGENCY NOT MADE OUT ON THE PLEADED FACTS

Institution without completing pre-institution mediation exposes the plaint to rejection under Order VII Rule 11. A recital of urgency without supporting facts does not satisfy the proviso.
